## Preface: Structure as the Subject

Most painters use structure to hold a subject. Piet Mondrian made structure the subject.

By about 1921 he had reduced painting to black lines meeting at right angles, rectangles filled with white, gray or one of the three primary colors, and nothing else. The National Gallery of Art describes the horizontal and vertical elements of these compositions as calibrated to produce a balanced asymmetry, forces in opposition that parallel a dynamic equilibrium in nature. The phrase is Mondrian's own preoccupation, and the museum's audio guide notes that he liked to talk about it.

Dynamic equilibrium is a useful phrase for anyone who governs an enterprise. An enterprise is a set of distinct and sometimes opposing elements that must hold together as a coherent whole while remaining able to move. The governance question is never whether there is structure. It is whether the structure is the right structure, visible to those who must live inside it, and whether it can be changed without the whole coming apart.

That is why Mondrian is worth a module. Not because black lines look like controls. They do, and the resemblance is the least interesting thing about them. He is worth a module because his mature work demonstrates that a very small number of elements, held in disciplined relation, can carry an enormous amount of meaning, and because his career demonstrates that the simplicity was arrived at, not assumed. Both of those are claims this method makes about itself.

## I. The Canvas Is the Enterprise

Take the organization as a canvas.

On it are people, technologies, capital, information, obligations, ambitions and risks. Left alone, these do not compose themselves. They form a field of competing interests, some loud, some quiet and some invisible until something fails. Governance is what supplies the composition: the decision about what goes where, what touches what and what is kept apart.

Artificial intelligence arrives on this canvas as a new color. The temptation is to add it where there is room and leave the rest alone. The method's first proposition is that this does not work. A new color changes the balance of everything already on the canvas, and a composition that was in equilibrium before the color arrived may not be in equilibrium afterwards. The relationships have to be redrawn, not merely extended.

The challenge is therefore stated at the outset in Mondrian's terms rather than in the technology's: preserve coherence while introducing a new capability. The rest of the module is about how.

---

## II. The Lines: Informed Intent

Mondrian's black lines are neither incidental nor decorative. They define where one plane ends and another begins, and they define it before the color is applied. Nothing on the canvas is a shape until the lines have made it one.

In this account those lines are drawn by **Informed Intent**, as stated in DOCTRINE.md: no AI system acts without prior authorization stating its purpose, authority, boundaries and exit, given by a human who meets the five authorizer conditions. The four elements of the instrument are the four kinds of line a composition needs. Purpose says what the plane is for. Authority says who may draw it. Boundaries say where it stops. Exit says how it is removed or redrawn when it must be. An authorization missing its exit is a plane with three sides, and the doctrine's own word for that is a hope.

The five conditions on the authorizer, Knowledge, Evidence, Authority, Time and Permission, are what distinguish a line drawn by a painter from a line drawn by a ruler held by someone who did not look. The doctrine's judgment is blunt: an instrument carrying all four elements, signed by a human lacking any of the five conditions, is paperwork. Its opposite is **Inferred Intent**, the purpose the authorizer never stated, supplied afterwards by the system or its vendor. In Mondrian's terms that is a line painted in after the color has already spread, to make it look as if someone had meant it.

One correction to the usual reading of constraint follows. Lines do not diminish freedom on this canvas. They are what make freedom usable, because without them there is no plane for a color to be free within. A painter who refuses all constraints may produce a masterpiece. An enterprise that refuses all constraints is more likely to produce an incident report.

## III. The Relationships: Slow AI

The colored planes are the enterprise's capabilities, activities and outcomes. AI adds a great deal of color to the palette and promises more. But a composition is not improved by the quantity of color on it. It is improved, or ruined, by the relationships among the planes: their proportion, their adjacency, their weight against one another and against the white.

This is where **Slow AI** does its work, as stated in DOCTRINE.md: AI that cannot be governed, explained and audited is not deployed; the three tests are conjunctive, and the doctrine is a precondition, not a speed limit. The three tests are tests of relationship. Governed means the plane has a named owner, defined boundaries and a working intervention path, which is to say that it stands in a known relation to a human and to its neighbors. Explained means the organization can say why the plane is where it is, in terms the affected person can use. Audited means the evidence of all this exists before anyone asks for it. A plane that passes two tests and fails the third is not two-thirds governed. It is out of the composition.

Here a distinction the module particularly wants to keep: the lines are controls, but the relationships between lines and colors are governance. Compliance asks whether the color stays within the lines. Governance asks whether the lines, the colors and their relationships produce a defensible whole. A compliant rectangle is not necessarily a well-governed rectangle, because the question is never the rectangle alone. It is the rectangle's place in the composition. The method requires both, and the Restaurant addendum exists because the second does not follow from the first.

Slow AI does not require the enterprise to move slowly. It requires that action not outrun the organization's capacity to see, state and evidence the relationships that action changes. Mondrian's planes do not move at all, and his compositions are nonetheless charged with motion. The motion is in the relationships. So is the governance.

## IV. The White: Human Judgment

This may be the most important part of the analogy, and it is the part most often lost when the paintings are reduced to a pattern for a dress or a building facade.

The white in a Mondrian is not unpainted canvas and it is not background. The National Gallery of Art's description of its lozenge composition notes that one complete square at the center is painted white, and that the others are each a slightly different white, with hints of pale blue and gray. The white is composed. It balances the lines and the colors, and the paintings would collapse without it.

In the enterprise, the white is human judgment, and more exactly the room in which judgment can be exercised. The ability to pause. To question. To reject a recommendation that is fluent and wrong. To demand better evidence. To decide that something should not be done at all. Not every available space on the canvas should be filled with color, and not every available decision should be filled with automation. The capacity to act includes the capacity to refrain from acting, and an organization that automates every decision has also automated away its opportunity to reconsider any of them. The resulting composition may be very efficient. It may also be impossible to live with.

In the method, the white is where the gates are. Gate 2, the manual first pass, is white space deliberately kept before any color is applied. Gate 6, verification, is white space kept between the color and the frame. Gate 7, the human release decision, is the last white before the painting leaves the studio. Slow AI protects the white not because the white is where the value is, but because the white is where the value is judged.

## V. The Authorship: Final Liability

The temptation at this point is to give **Final Liability** a rectangle of its own, perhaps a red one in the corner. The module resists it. Final Liability is not an element in the composition. It is the authorship of the composition.

DOCTRINE.md states it: Final Liability rests with the Human: every material outcome attaches to a named human owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene. The three attributes are what distinguish an author from a signature. A name in the corner of a canvas painted by someone else is a forgery, and the doctrine says the same of governance: a person who is named but holds none of the three attributes is a nominee, and nominating one is worse than naming nobody because it manufactures the appearance of accountability.

The model may contribute color, structure and even proposals for rearrangement. It may draft the composition and argue for it. It does not become the author, because authorship in this account is not a matter of who did the most work. It is a matter of who can be held to the result: named at specification, attaching at confirmation and holding through reliance, for as long as anyone depends on what left the studio.

A drafting caution belongs here, and DOCTRINE.md section 3.2 supplies it. Final Liability is a governance doctrine about where accountability must sit. It is not a claim that every legal liability for an AI outcome attaches personally to one individual, and a module that implied so would be doing the thing the drafting rule forbids, using liability language where legal liability does not in fact land. The Human of this doctrine owns the composition. What the law does with that ownership is a separate and jurisdiction-specific question, and the doctrine's statutory analogues are cited there as analogues, not as the doctrine itself.

## VI. The Recomposition: GRCnext™

Mondrian did not stop at the grid he perfected in the 1920s. The Museum of Modern Art's note on Broadway Boogie Woogie (1942 to 1943) records that the painting omits black altogether and breaks what had been uniform bars of color into multicolored segments, after a first evening in New York at which he was introduced to boogie-woogie, whose "continuous opposition of pure means" he took to be analogous to his own. The composition is still a composition. It has been recomposed.

This is the register in which **GRCnext™** is read here. Traditional governance, risk management and compliance asks whether the risks were identified, documented and controlled. GRCnext™ asks what choices the enterprise will still have when the risk happens, and whether those choices can be executed within 72 hours. Governance cannot be limited to maintaining the original composition. It must preserve the organization's ability to change the composition when circumstances demand it, without losing its identity, breaching its obligations or surrendering control.

The five primitives supply the grammar of recomposition:

| GRCnext™ primitive | Read on the canvas |
|---|---|
| Services | The outcomes the composition exists to sustain; what must still be true of the painting after it is rearranged |
| Tolerances | How far a plane may shift, shrink or change color before the composition is no longer the one that was authorized |
| Pipes | The dependencies connecting planes to one another and to what lies beyond the frame |
| Switches | The authorized points at which a plane can be moved, isolated or repainted while the painting is on the wall |
| Exits | The ability to remove a plane, replace it or take the painting down, built before it is needed |

The 72-hour test then asks whether these are real: whether the switch can be thrown and the exit taken under pressure, by the people who will be there when the pressure arrives. A contingency plan that cannot be executed under stress is not optionality. It is documentation.

Mondrian's lozenge compositions add one more thing. The National Gallery of Art records that he said the diamond compositions were about cutting, and in them the lines and planes run off the edge of the canvas, so that the structure plainly continues beyond what the viewer can see. For an enterprise that is the right picture of Pipes. Suppliers, platforms, financial channels and jurisdictions do not stop mattering because they lie outside the formal perimeter. GRCnext™ makes what is beyond the frame part of the design, because the frame is where most compositions fail.

## VII. Where the Analogy Breaks

An analogy is only as useful as its known limits. This one has three.

**First, the black.** Broadway Boogie Woogie removes the black lines entirely. If the lines are Informed Intent and the controls of Slow AI, the most attractive moment in the analogy is also the moment it threatens to say something false: that a mature enterprise may recompose itself until the controls disappear from view. It may not. Slow AI's three tests are conjunctive and do not relax with maturity. The module therefore reads Broadway Boogie Woogie more carefully than the first impression allows. The grid has not gone. Look at the painting and the verticals and horizontals are everywhere; what has changed is that they are now made of the colored segments themselves rather than of a separate black armature. That is a recognizable governance state: controls carried by the activities rather than fenced around them, the service ticket inside the agent run rather than the policy beside it. It is a legitimate and sometimes superior arrangement. It passes the doctrine only if the grid is still there to be seen, stated and evidenced. The auditor's question in front of a mature deployment is the viewer's question in front of Broadway Boogie Woogie: can you still see the grid. If the answer is no, the enterprise has not recomposed its governance. It has exited it, without a Switch and without an Exit.

**Second, the harmony.** Mondrian's project was, by his own account and the museums', a spiritual one: the paintings were meant to express a conviction that universal harmonies preside in nature. An enterprise has no such warrant. Its equilibrium is contingent, provisional and legal, held together by contracts, regulators, markets and the patience of the people inside it, and it must be tested against reality on a schedule the enterprise does not set. This module borrows Mondrian's compositional discipline. It borrows none of his metaphysics. Where the analogy invites a reader to believe that a well-composed governance arrangement is harmonious in some deeper sense, the reader should decline the invitation and run the 72-hour test instead.

**Third, the author.** Mondrian was the sole author of his canvases and owed the composition to no one. He could remove the black because the only person who had to live with the result was himself. An enterprise is composed by many hands, authorized by some of them, relied on by people who never saw the studio and answerable to others who did not consent to the arrangement at all. Final Liability is stated at the strength it is because the enterprise has no single painter. The doctrine supplies one by naming one.

These limits do not weaken the analogy. They are what make it safe to use.

## VIII. Scroll to Decompose: The Method of Arriving

The compositions are the outcome. The more useful lesson for a method is how Mondrian arrived at them, and for that there is a better guide than a museum label.

In July 2026 the designer Onofrio Napolitano published *A Tribute to Piet Mondrian* at [mondriantribute.com](https://www.mondriantribute.com/), an interactive WebGL experience that walks the visitor through Mondrian's development from his early landscapes through Neoplastic abstraction to the rhythm of his New York work. Awwwards recognized it with an Honorable Mention on 22 July 2026 ([awwwards.com/sites/a-tribute-to-piet-mondrian](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/a-tribute-to-piet-mondrian)). The site's own introduction describes Mondrian's life as a search for balance pursued by stripping every superfluous thing from the forms he painted, and its homepage carries a three-word instruction that this module borrows gratefully: scroll to decompose. The credit for the phrase, the design and the idea of showing the evolution as motion rather than as a sequence of stills belongs to its author. The reading of it below is the present author's.

The instruction is apt because Mondrian did not begin with rectangles. He began with trees. The paintings held by Kunstmuseum Den Haag show the sequence: the trees of 1908 to 1910 still recognizably trees, then *Gray Tree* (1911) with its branches becoming a lattice of curves under the influence of Cubism, then *Flowering Apple Tree* (1912), in which the tree has nearly become the lattice. Observation came first. Abstraction was reached by decomposing what had been observed into the relationships that mattered and discarding the rest, and the discarding took years.

That sequence is the Kitchen's sequence, and it is worth stating plainly because the method is sometimes mistaken for a set of restrictions on prompting.

1. **Observe the landscape.** Begin with the real organization: people, power, incentives, workflows, services, dependencies and consequences. Understand what is there before deciding what ought to be. This is Step 1 and Step 4, Define the Dish and Read the Kitchen.
2. **Decompose into essential relationships.** Identify purpose, authority, boundaries, evidence, acceptable risk and exit, by hand, before the machine is in the room. Remove procedural complexity without removing the information judgment needs. This is Informed Intent at work in Step 2 and Step 3, the Definition of Done and Knife Work: the tree drawn and redrawn until only the lattice remains.
3. **Build the disciplined composition.** Give the relationships operational form through bounded AI use, decision rights, records and verification. This is Slow AI at work in Steps 5, 7 and 8, Apply Heat, Build the Plate and Taste Before Service.
4. **Preserve authorship.** The composition has a named owner with decision rights, oversight and the power to intervene, from the first step and not only at the last. This is Final Liability at Step 6 and Step 9, confirmed at the Chef de Partie and exercised at the Pass.
5. **Recompose when circumstances change.** Services, Tolerances, Pipes, Switches and Exits keep the choices executable as the environment moves. This is the Feast, Steps 10 to 12, where the registry, the brigade and the review cycle are the Switches and Exits of an organization rather than of a task.

Simplicity, on this reading, is an achievement rather than a starting assumption. To simplify responsibly one must first understand what is being simplified. That is the concern underneath Informed Intent, and it is the risk underneath every fluent AI output: the system can reduce a complicated situation to an elegant answer, and if the reduction discards material context, uncertainty, authority or consequence, the elegance is the danger. The method asks a more disciplined question than what can be removed. It asks what must be preserved for the Human to remain informed and in control. Mondrian kept the relationships. So must we.

## IX. Making Transformation Visible

The tribute's central design idea is to make the transformation visible: the viewer experiences the transitions between Mondrian's states, so the final image is never separated from the process that produced it. A static image of the 1921 grid tells you what he arrived at. The scroll tells you how.

Governance has the same two registers, and most compliance regimes live only in the first.

| A static compliance view asks | The method asks |
|---|---|
| What does the system look like now? | How did it arrive at this state, and who authorized each change? |
| Does a control exist? | Does the control work, and is there evidence that it worked before anyone asked? |
| Is someone designated to approve? | Can that person make an informed decision and intervene, under the five authorizer conditions? |
| Is the current arrangement acceptable? | What happens when it changes or fails? |
| Is there a documented contingency? | Can the organization execute it within 72 hours? |

The comparison is conceptual. An animation, however good, demonstrates nothing about auditability, and the present author has not tested the site's full interaction sequence in a controlled setting. What it demonstrates is a design principle the method shares: show the change, not only the result. The method adds what the enterprise requires beyond that: authorization before the change, evidence of it, a named owner through it and the ability to stop or reverse it.

## X. Dynamic Equilibrium

Bring the pieces together and the composition is simple to state.

Informed Intent draws the lines. Slow AI disciplines the relationships. Final Liability names the author. GRCnext™ preserves the ability to recompose. The Slow AI Kitchen and the Origami Method are how people are taught to compose: define the task, draw the boundaries, name the owner, test the arrangement and keep the evidence before anything goes into operation.

What recommends Mondrian above other analogies this account has used is that he achieved complexity through simplicity. His mature compositions use remarkably few elements, and their sophistication lies entirely in the relationships among them. This method attempts the same: to reduce an extraordinarily complicated problem of enterprise governance to a small number of principles that people can understand, apply, verify and improve, and to insist that the principles are not the point. The composition is.

The analogy also carries its own warning, and the warning is doctrinal. Too much constraint destroys adaptability; the enterprise cannot recompose. Too little destroys coherence; the enterprise cannot be audited. Dynamic equilibrium is not reached by the mechanical application of rules at either extreme. It is reached by judgment, which is to say by the white, and the white is Human.

The objective is neither rigid control nor unrestricted freedom. It is sufficient structure to preserve coherence, sufficient freedom to permit progress and sufficient Human authority to change the composition when it must change. Governance, read this way, is the art of creating freedom through deliberate structure. The art is in the relationships. The responsibility is in the Human.

A well-governed enterprise, like a well-made Mondrian, is more than the sum of its squares.

## Where the Composition Lands in the Method

The module adds no discipline and no gate. It supplies a picture of the method as a whole, and the picture maps onto the method as it stands.

**The lines** are Gates 1, 4 and 5. Step 2 (Mise en Place) draws the Definition of Done before any color is applied. Step 5 (Apply Heat) confines the AI to the planes the lines define. Step 6 (Name the Chef de Partie) confirms that the lines were drawn by someone entitled to draw them.

**The white** is Gates 2, 6 and 7. Step 3 (Knife Work) keeps the human's first pass clear of the machine. Step 8 (Taste Before Service) is verification performed against something outside the model. Step 9 (Open the Pass) is the release decision, and the whiteboard test at that step is the viewer standing in front of the painting and explaining why every plane is where it is, with no model in the room.

**The authorship** is Step 1 and Gate 5 together: the Owner named at Define the Dish and confirmed at the Chef de Partie, holding through reliance, with the Service Record as the signature on the finished canvas.

**The recomposition** is the Feast. Step 10 (Plan the Menu) is the registry of planes the organization has authorized. Step 11 (Staff the Brigade) is the set of hands permitted to touch them. Step 12 (Run the Event) is the review cycle in which the composition is tested, and ten Service Records trigger a method review, which is the organization's own scroll to decompose.

**The method of arriving** in Section VIII is the twelve steps in their existing order, read as observation, decomposition, composition, authorship and recomposition. It changes no step, gate, role or principle.

## A Note on Provenance

In the spirit of Coetzee's Kitchen, the lineage of this module is stated, and the statement begins with a confession.

I have always had a difficult time understanding art. I have been to a great many museums, and my favorite part of most of them was the shop. This is a vast chasm in my education, and I am trying, late, to fill it: with AI, with YouTube, with art books and with help from my Wife and Daughter, who both studied art formally and who are patient with the questions. A reader who knows Mondrian will find nothing new here about Mondrian. What is here is what a lawyer and auditor found when he finally looked.

The reading itself began as an interpretation produced in conversation with an AI system, in two passes, the second after the interactive tribute was brought into the conversation. It was then rebuilt with a second AI system and tested against the museum sources cited below. In that test the draft's treatment of Broadway Boogie Woogie as a clean metaphor for recomposition failed, for the reason Section VII gives, and the doctrines were restated in the forms DOCTRINE.md permits. The limits in Section VII and the mapping onto the method were added. The analogy is retained because it survived; where it did not, it was changed.

Cognitive Provenance asks which conclusions are the Human's and which arrived from the machine. The honest answer is that the canvas was the machine's suggestion, that I would not have reached for Mondrian unaided, and that the grid, the white, the breaks and the mapping are mine. Whether that is a satisfactory division is exactly the question Coetzee's Kitchen says to keep asking.
