## The thesis the work serves

An outcome costs what it takes of its owner's attention, and no more outcomes can be accepted than the owner can stand behind. Two things the owner holds keep AI useful, cost-effective and proper: the definition of done and the power to stop.

In the author's words, from his LinkedIn posts of November 2026: "That is Lean AI: the power to intervene, improved by AI and not overwhelmed by it. AI should take busywork away from the Human, not manufacture more of it to check." And: "Lean AI is not about headcount. It is about a better process." The thesis sentence puts those lines in terms that can be counted.

The module and the paper are one calculation seen from two sides. The module counts the cost of an accepted outcome: model and tool charges, the effort of producing the work and the effort of overseeing it, divided by the outcomes accepted. The paper counts the oversight that an owner's workflows demand and sets it beside the hours that owner can give. The oversight effort in the first is the demand in the second, priced. The same minutes sit in both.

This is a hypothesis. The only figures behind it so far are the invented ones in the module's illustration and in the paper's worked example. Each Lean Tiny Servant reports what share of its cost per accepted outcome was oversight, so that its runs can count against the claim as well as for it.

## The rules of the work

1. **One unit a week, on Wednesdays** (Korea Standard Time, KST). A unit is one chapter, one stage of one servant or one revision of the module.
2. **One unit in progress at a time.** A unit that is not finished carries to the next week, and nothing new starts until it ships.
3. **The author reads every unit before it ships.** A unit he has not read does not ship. A skipped week is skipped, not doubled.
4. **Nothing here claims a saving.** A savings claim needs a pilot run under the [pilot protocol](pilot-protocol.md) and reported whatever its result.
5. **Every servant is tested, and its test record is kept,** failures included. The records are the book's appendices.
6. **The author keeps a log of his own oversight:** the minutes he spends reading, correcting and approving each unit, and whether he accepted it at the first read. It is published in this folder from unit 1 as self-administered evidence from one owner, and as nothing more.
7. **A case drawn from Client work appears only with the Client's written consent,** anonymized and as derived figures. No Client data is kept in this repository.
8. **Twelve Wednesdays make a cycle,** and the twelfth is a review of the plan against the log.

Lean AI adds no doctrine, step, gate, tier, role or rule to the Kitchen. The [doctrine canon](https://github.com/rolldabones/rolldabones/blob/main/DOCTRINE.md) governs.
